Release notes

Every published version of the Open24 platform — combined frontend and backend. Each card is a self-contained summary of what changed and when. Copy a version link to share or to filter the page to just that release.

v1.6.1 July 30, 2026

Reserve page: easier court switching on mobile

  • Practice / Full Court switch is always visible. On mobile, the switch between booking a single court and booking the entire facility now stays on screen no matter where you scroll, instead of only appearing once you scrolled down.
  • One-tap court switching. Choosing a different court while in Practice Court mode is now a single tap on a scrollable row of court names, instead of opening a dropdown and picking from a list.
  • Consistent "Full Court" labelling. Fixed a few remaining spots that still showed "Full-Court" with a hyphen — every screen now consistently reads "Full Court".

Coach notification emails

  • Fixed: coach assignment emails sometimes missing student names. When a coach was assigned to a course, or to an on-demand lesson booked by the head coach, the notification email could arrive without the list of enrolled students. Both cases are now fixed so coaches always see who's on their roster.
v1.6.0 July 24, 2026

Book the entire facility in one reservation

  • New "Book the entire facility" mode. On the Reserve page you can now switch between booking a single court and booking the whole facility (every court) in one go — perfect for tournaments, camps, and private events. A simple toggle at the top swaps between the two modes.
  • No more double-bookings. Reserving the entire facility automatically blocks all of its individual courts for that time, and any existing court booking blocks the whole-facility option — so a full-facility reservation and a single-court booking can never overlap on the same space.
  • Honest, real-time availability. Whichever mode you're in, the time grid reflects what's actually free: full-facility slots grey out the moment any court is taken, and courts grey out when the facility is booked as a whole.
  • Mobile-friendly. The mode switch and court picker are tuned for phones — a full-width toggle with condensed labels and a compact, scroll-aware header so you can book on the go.
  • Admin tools updated throughout. Staff can set each resource's booking scope (single court vs. entire facility) and see the scope clearly on resource lists, bookings, time-slot management, and transactions.

Passes and promo codes that know which court they're for

  • Court-typed passes. A pass is now marked as either a Practice Court pass or a Full Court pass. A Practice Court pass may only be used to book an individual court, and a Full Court pass may only be used to book the entire facility — no accidental mismatches.
  • One checkout, multiple passes. If your cart mixes a practice court and a full court, and you own the matching passes for both, checkout now applies each pass to its eligible item in a single order — and shows the units used per pass in the totals.
  • Court-typed promo codes. Admins can restrict a promo code to Any Court, Practice Court, or Full Court. When you apply a court-restricted code to a mixed cart, the discount is now applied only to the eligible items — no more "sorry, doesn't apply" for the whole cart.
  • Clearer promo feedback. Applying an ineligible promo code now shows a friendly amber "Coupon not applied" message with the reason, instead of a scary red error.
  • Consistent court-type labels. "Full Court" and "Practice Court" badges are now used everywhere — in the cart, on pass templates, in admin promotion lists, and on booking summaries — so it's always obvious which court a booking / pass / promo is for.

Clearer, more accurate refunds and payment statuses

  • Fixed: rescheduled time-slots showed a $0 refund line. Refund breakdowns now show the correct amount for slots that came from a reschedule, instead of $0.
  • New "Partially Refunded" status. When only some of a booking's time-slots are refunded, the payment status now reads Partially Refunded rather than a full "Refunded" — on both the customer's booking page and the admin transactions list.
  • Payment method stays visible after a refund. A refunded card booking now still shows "Card" as the payment method instead of a blank dash.
  • Reschedule upcharge clarity. When a booking's reschedule fee was paid on a separate charge, the refund summary now explains that the figures reflect the original payment and the upcharge is refunded from its own transaction.
v1.5.0 July 18, 2026

Marketing Campaigns — send targeted emails to members, straight from Admin

  • New Admin ▸ Marketing ▸ Campaigns. Compose a marketing email (subject, preview text, rich HTML body), pick an audience, and send. Three audience modes: All customers (everyone who hasn't opted out), Filtered (by membership status, role, pass-holder, join-date range, name/email search), and Manual (paste a specific list of customer IDs).
  • Preview the recipient count before you send. Filters update a live count so you know exactly how many people you're about to email. Send a test to yourself first, then schedule immediately or for a future date/time.
  • Live per-recipient send tracking. The campaign detail page shows every recipient's status in real time — sent, failed, unsubscribed, pending — with a classified reason for any failure (e.g. mailbox full, invalid address, sender quota exceeded). One-click Retry failed for transient failures.
  • Pause and resume mid-send. Made a typo mid-campaign? Pause the send, cancel the campaign, or (for large sends that hit our daily Gmail limit) the system auto-pauses and shows an "acknowledge and resume when quota resets" banner.
  • One-click unsubscribe in every marketing email. Every marketing email now carries a CASL / CAN-SPAM compliant footer and Gmail's one-click unsubscribe button — customers can opt out of marketing with a single click without having to log in. Their opt-out is instant and applies to every future marketing send.
  • Marketing opt-out is marketing-only. Unsubscribing from marketing has zero effect on transactional email — booking confirmations, refund receipts, pass expiries, and coach notifications keep flowing as they always did.

Coaches now get an email when they're assigned to (or removed from) a course

  • Course-level assignment notifications. When Admin assigns a coach to a course or activity (or unassigns them), that coach now receives an email confirming the change, with the course name, schedule, and a link to their dashboard.
  • Session-level assignment notifications. The same coach-facing email fires when a single session's coach is changed (e.g. covering for another coach on one date), so both the outgoing and incoming coach know about the swap without a manual heads-up from Admin.

Pass expiry now shows the correct end date on seasonal passes

  • Fixed: seasonal passes bought during the season sometimes showed the wrong expiry. For pass templates with a fixed end date (e.g. a March–September summer pass), the expiry shown on the customer's pass, the admin pass detail page, and the purchase confirmation email now always match the template's actual end date. Previously, edge cases in the backend could store an expiry based on days from purchase instead, causing the emailed expiry to disagree with what the customer saw in their account.
  • Admin views make expiry derivation explicit. Both the pass template detail page and each individual pass detail page now clearly label whether an expiry is a Fixed end date or N days after purchase — so admins can immediately explain to a customer why a given pass expires when it does.

Pass confirmation emails now show the exact amount paid, including member discounts

  • Fixed: member-priced pass confirmations showed the non-member subtotal. When a member bought a pass at their member-only price, the confirmation email's "Amount Paid" line was rendering the template's full non-member subtotal instead of the discounted amount they were actually charged. The email now reads the exact amount from the Stripe charge for every purchase flow (checkout redirect and saved-card), so the receipt agrees with the card statement to the penny.
v1.4.0 July 15, 2026

Member pricing — visible everywhere members book, enroll, and pay

  • See the member price up front while browsing. On Enroll and Events & Camps, courses that offer a member price now show it in emerald with the regular per-session rate crossed out beside it and a −X% savings badge. Everyone sees this — including guests and non-members — so prospective members can see exactly how much a membership would save before they commit.
  • Mobile browse layout tuned for the new price tile. On phones, when a course has a member price the stat card gets its own row so the strikethrough and discount badge don't get crammed against the other stats; the remaining two info tiles then split the row below evenly. Courses without a member price keep the pre-existing compact 3-column layout.
  • Enrollment detail — Total Due now shows the actual price you paid. On My Enrollments, when you enrolled at the member price the Payment card now displays the emerald member total, the non-member equivalent struck through, and a Member −X% pill — matching the checkout amount to the penny.
  • Legacy on-demand enrollments auto-heal. A handful of on-demand enrollments booked before the flag was introduced were showing the non-member total even though members were actually charged the member price. Those enrollments now recognize the member payment and display correctly without needing a data migration.
  • Confirmation emails match the price you paid. The Enrollment Confirmed email's Amount Paid row now shows the emerald member amount alongside the strikethrough regular amount and the Member −X% savings pill — so the receipt on the confirmation email agrees with what the buyer sees in the app and on their card statement.
  • Admin and coach on-demand scheduling now honor member pricing. Previously, when an admin or head coach scheduled a member's on-demand request into a session, the resulting enrollment silently reverted to the non-member rate, producing enrollments where the customer-visible Total Due disagreed with what they had already paid. Both the admin schedule flow and the head-coach schedule flow now persist the member price on the new enrollment — so the numbers match everywhere.
  • Transactions list — a subtle crown marks member payments. Desktop keeps the inline Member −X% pill next to the amount. On mobile, a small emerald crown icon now sits in front of the price so the Member indicator no longer clashes with the Succeeded / Refunded status badge.
  • Admin enrollment detail matches the customer view. The Total Amount Due tile on the admin enrollment detail page shows the same emerald member total + strikethrough non-member total + Member −X% pill, and the Total Amount Paid pill surfaces the discount percent alongside the "Member" label.
v1.3.4 July 8, 2026

Refunds — one student's cancellation no longer cancels the session for everyone

  • Fixed: refunding a session sometimes wiped that session off the schedule. When an admin refunded a student's spot in a session where that student happened to be the only one currently registered, the session itself was being marked as cancelled — freeing its court/coach slot and quietly emailing any future enrollees. Refunds now only remove that student's attendance; the session stays on the schedule and remains bookable by other students.
  • Enrollment auto-cancel still works as intended. If every one of an enrollment's active sessions gets refunded, the enrollment itself is still automatically marked Cancelled. That per-enrollment behavior is unchanged — only the cross-enrollment session cascade was removed.

Enrollment page — refund amount and payment method now match reality

  • Fixed: partial refund shown as far larger than it actually was. When a customer had a single session refunded, the "Refunded" line on their enrollment page could display the full session-pack price plus the wrong methods ("$37.29 · card + store credit" when the actual refund was one session paid to store credit only). The summary now reads the session-by-session refund total and derives the destination from the actual credit-transactions on file, so the amount and the "· card / store credit" label always match what the customer will see on their card statement or in their store credit balance.
  • Same fix applied to the Admin enrollments list. The Refunded X · method pill on the Admin ▸ Course Enrollments list and the "Refund Total" column in its CSV export used the same broken formula. Both now agree with the enrollment detail page — a 1-of-3 session refund shows one session's worth, not all three.

Enrollment page — Cancelled vs. Upcoming badges now clearly different

  • At-a-glance session status. On My Enrollments, the Cancelled session badge used to be a pale red pill that looked very similar to the pale blue Upcoming pill — several customers mentioned mistaking a cancelled session for a normal one. Cancelled is now a bold filled red pill with a "prohibited" icon; Upcoming is now a green outline pill. In-Progress and Concluded are unchanged.

Admin course detail page — cleaner, faster to scan, and mobile-friendly

  • Enrolled Students count fixed. Cancelled enrollments were being counted alongside active ones, so the "Enrolled Students" number stayed the same after a refund. Cancelled and withdrawn students no longer inflate the count. A new "Show cancelled (N)" toggle reveals them on demand for auditing.
  • Redesigned header, stats, and actions. Course title with a compact copy-ID pill, matching badge pills for cohort / level / schedule / lesson type / inactive, and lighter Delete + Edit Course buttons in the same style as the other page actions.
  • New Course Sessions section. The month calendar is now hidden by default (admins told us they rarely use it day-to-day) and can be opened via a "Show calendar" button — the choice is remembered per admin. The primary list toggles cleanly between compact cards and a dense table. On phones the default is cards; on desktop it defaults to the table — whatever you pick sticks.

Admin courses list — enrolled count now matches the detail page

  • Fixed: cancelled students inflated the "N enrolled" tile. On Admin ▸ Courses, each course card's "enrolled" number counted every enrollment on the course — including those marked Cancelled or Withdrawn. That disagreed with the course detail page's filtered count (which correctly excludes them) and could incorrectly flip the "Full" badge on a course that actually had open spots. The list card now uses the same filter as the detail page, so both numbers agree and the "Full" badge only shows when the course is genuinely at capacity.

Enroll page — cancelled enrollments no longer make a course look "Full"

  • Fixed: available courses, events, and camps displayed as "Full" on the customer enroll page. A course with capacity 5 that had 4 active enrollments and 1 cancelled was being shown as Full to customers on Enroll, blocking quick-enroll and pushing them to the waiting list. The availability calculator was counting cancelled and withdrawn enrollments against the capacity. It now excludes them everywhere the same signal is read — the customer enroll page, the Events & Camps page (Join), the admin capacity warnings on the resource-calendar enroll dialog and the manage-enrollments page, and the coach/admin table views — so Full only appears when the course is genuinely at capacity, and the "N spots left" chip reflects real availability. The coach resource-calendar's semi-private "enrolled names" chip list also hides cancelled students.
v1.3.3 July 5, 2026

Admin custom email — live progress, cancel, and no more page hangs

  • See it happen in real time. Sending a custom email to a large group used to leave the page frozen with a spinner until every message had gone out — sometimes tens of minutes with no feedback. The page now shows a live progress bar with a running count (Sending 42 / 247), which batch is in flight, an estimated time remaining, and the most-recently delivered address.
  • Cancel mid-send. Change of plans? Hit Cancel and the send stops at the next batch boundary — batches already dispatched still deliver, and the result screen shows exactly how many went out before the stop.
  • No accidental interruptions. Closing or refreshing the page while a send is in progress now prompts a confirmation — so a stray browser click doesn't cut the blast short.
  • Honest results screen. When a send finishes, the summary clearly indicates whether every recipient received the message (green ✔), whether some failed (amber, with a per-recipient reason list of up to 20 failures + link to full email logs), or whether the operator cancelled (with the exact "X of Y" count).
  • Works the same at any scale. A head coach sending to a 20-student roster gets the same responsive UX as a full-database announcement to a thousand customers.
  • Personalization tokens now styled in the subject preview. Placeholder tokens like {{firstName}} in the email subject now show the same blue dashed-outline pill treatment as in the email body preview — so it's clear at a glance which parts of your subject line will be personalized per recipient.

Email deliverability — bulk sends no longer trigger Gmail throttling

  • Fixed: a bulk send that stopped after ~90 messages. A recent 1000-recipient blast was interrupted mid-run when the outbound mail relay rejected further messages as "too many login attempts". The underlying cause — the mail service opening a fresh authenticated session for every single message instead of reusing one — has been reworked: one persistent connection is now kept open for the whole run, and outbound sends are paced automatically. Large sends now complete cleanly without tripping any relay-side limit.
  • Automatic retry on transient hiccups. If the mail relay does return a temporary error mid-send (rate limit, connection reset, timeout), the message is retried with a growing backoff instead of being dropped — up to a few retries. Combined with the connection-reuse change, this means transient network blips no longer show up as failed rows in the email log.

Admin email logs — much faster page load

  • Same list, a fraction of the data. The Admin ▸ Email Logs page was loading each row with the entire HTML body of the email attached to it — for pages containing long marketing emails, that meant a megabyte or two of payload per page just to display the summary table. The list now transfers only the columns actually shown (subject, recipient, status, sent-at). The full email content still loads on demand when you open a specific log entry.
  • Long subjects no longer stretch the table. Personalized bulk-send subjects (which can be long once each recipient's name is filled in) are now truncated in the list view with the full text available on hover — the table keeps a consistent width.

Reliability — passes with more than 10 sessions no longer silently fail

  • Fixed: a pass purchase that took payment but created no pass. A customer buying a 20-session pass had their card charged successfully but no pass appeared on their account and no confirmation email was sent. The pass storage layer still had an old cap of 10 sessions per pass, even though the pass-template layer was updated in v1.3.2 to allow any bundle size. The cap has been removed everywhere — pass bundles of 20, 25, or any positive size now save correctly on purchase. The affected customer's pass has been manually issued.
v1.3.2 July 4, 2026

Members save on passes — new member pricing

  • Two prices, one pass. Admins can now set a separate Member Price on any pass template alongside the regular (non-member) price. Members who are signed in see the discounted price applied automatically at checkout — no coupon code, no manual adjustment.
  • Discount shown everywhere it matters. On the Buy Pass page, the pass detail card, and the checkout screen, member pricing is shown with the regular price crossed out, the discounted price highlighted, and a "save X%" badge — so members can see exactly how much their membership saved them.
  • Non-members see the perk too. Guests browsing passes see a preview line — "Members pay $X.XX (save Y%)" — so the value of membership is visible on the shopping surface itself.
  • Optional per pass. Leaving Member Price blank keeps the old behaviour — everyone pays the same price. Admins opt-in per template.

Passes — fixed end date + larger bundles

  • Choose how your pass expires. When creating or editing a pass, admins can now pick between two expiry modes: a rolling window ("valid for X days after purchase") or a fixed end date ("all passes end on Dec 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM"). Fixed end dates are ideal for seasonal passes — every buyer's pass ends on the same day regardless of when they bought it.
  • Bigger bundles. Removed the previous 10-pass ceiling on how many sessions a pass bundle can contain. Admins can now sell 20-packs, 25-packs, or any positive size. Quick-set chips for 10 and 20 keep the common cases fast to enter.
  • Editing the template updates existing passes. If you change a template's end date or validity period, every active pass sold from that template is automatically re-synced to the new rule. Admin-issued comps with a hand-picked expiry are left untouched.
  • Clearer expiry on all pass surfaces. The Buy Pass page, checkout, and My Passes page now show "Ends Dec 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM" for templates with a fixed end date, instead of the generic "180 days validity" text.

Admin — export bookings and course enrollments to CSV

  • One-click Excel export. The Admin ▸ Bookings and Admin ▸ Course Enrollments pages now have an Export CSV button that downloads the current filtered view as a spreadsheet-ready file. Filter the list first (by date, status, customer, search) and only the visible rows are exported.
  • Bookings export includes: booking ID, confirmation ID, created-at, state, type, customer name/email/phone, facility, resource names, slot count, first/last slot times, and price.
  • Enrollments export includes: enrollment ID, enrolled-at, status, enrollment type, customer + child name, course name, cohort, level, schedule type, sessions enrolled/attended/remaining, total due/paid/refunded, promotion codes, and payment confirmation IDs.
  • Excel-friendly out of the box. Files are UTF-8 with BOM and use local timestamps (e.g. 2026-07-04 14:30), so double-clicking opens them cleanly in Excel or Numbers with accented characters and dates intact.

Pass checkout — tax now shown on the confirmation

  • "Amount Paid" now includes tax on every pass purchase. When paying with a saved card or store credit, the pass confirmation screen previously showed only the pre-tax price. It now shows the full tax-inclusive amount that was actually charged, matching your credit-card statement and Stripe receipt to the penny.

Resource calendar — new "Bird View" for mobile admins

  • See every resource at once — now the default on mobile. Admins managing time slots on their phone now open the Resource Time Slots page directly into Bird View — a compact grid that shows every resource across the top and every half-hour of the day down the side, all in one screen. Scroll up and down to browse the day, scroll left and right to move between resources. Prefer the classic per-resource timeline? Flip the switch at the top of the page — your choice is remembered per device.
  • Same taps, same actions. Tapping any cell opens the same create / edit / book / enroll dialogs as the classic list view — nothing has been taken away.
  • Event blocks match the desktop view. Bird View cells now show the same customer name, course name, admin-booked marker, and unassociated-slot warnings as the desktop calendar (just without the start-end time, to fit the compact grid). Customer names deep-link to the customer's admin page; course names link to the course.
  • Steady headers while you scroll. Swiping up/down now moves only the schedule area — the resource-name header stays put. Swiping left/right moves only the resource columns — the time column stays put. No more disorienting diagonal drift on the two frozen headers.
  • Readable at density. Time labels are zero-padded so 12:00 AM and 2:00 AM line up perfectly; the time column is tinted differently from the schedule area; gentle row stripes make it easy to track a row across many columns; resource names in the top row are highlighted in the app's primary colour.

Polished mobile header

  • New page title on phones. The Resource Time Slots page now opens with a compact branded header — a gradient icon chip beside a two-line "Time Slots · Resource Availability" label — feeling like a proper app screen instead of a plain document heading. The desktop title is unchanged.
v1.3.1 June 28, 2026

Course enrollment reliability — payments always land

  • Fixed: payment succeeds but enrollment doesn't appear. A rare timing issue could cause the system to silently skip creating an enrollment record after a successful card charge — particularly on mobile or when using 3D-Secure bank verification. The customer saw a success screen but had no enrollment, no confirmation email, and no door access. This is now fixed: the checkout flow waits for all required data before finalizing, and any unexpected state surfaces an immediate error instead of failing silently. Full details →
  • Server-side safety net now active. A backup system that catches any missed enrollment (even if the customer's browser closes mid-checkout) was built but never connected to the payment processor due to a configuration oversight. It is now properly wired and will automatically recover any enrollment that the browser path misses — within seconds, with no manual intervention needed.

Admin tools — send targeted emails to course students

  • Select recipients by course. When composing a custom email from the admin panel, staff can now filter the recipient list to students enrolled in a specific course — then select all with one click. Useful for class announcements, schedule changes, or event reminders.
  • Personalize with names. Email subject and body now support {{firstName}}, {{lastName}}, and {{email}} placeholders — each recipient receives a personalized version. Example: "Hi {{firstName}}, your next session is…"
  • Branded email option. A toggle wraps your message in the standard Open24 branded header and footer — matching the look of automated platform emails — so custom announcements feel professional without needing to write full HTML.
v1.3.0 June 24, 2026

Phone numbers are now verified by text message

  • One-time code by SMS. Adding or changing a phone number anywhere in Open24 — at sign-up, when finishing a profile, or later from the profile page — now triggers a 6-digit code sent to that number, which must be entered before the change is saved. This prevents typos and look-alike numbers from entering the system, and means coaches and staff can trust that the phone number on file is reachable.
  • Verified badge. Phone numbers confirmed this way display a small green Verified badge. Legacy numbers (entered before this change) remain on file and show Unverified until the next confirmation — nothing breaks; the badge appears the next time the customer walks through any phone screen.
  • Honest status indicator. If the text-message provider is having issues, the screen says so up front instead of letting the customer burn through retries.

Change email or phone, securely

  • Change Email. The profile page now supports requesting an email change. A 6-digit code is sent to the new address — entering it proves control of the inbox and updates the account in one step. The sign-in email and display username stay perfectly in sync.
  • Change Phone Number. Same flow for the phone — a code is texted to the new number, and the account updates only after the code is entered.
  • Social sign-ins. Accounts signed in with Google or Facebook have their email managed by that provider, so it cannot be changed from inside Open24 (the profile page explains this in place of the change-email button). Changing the phone number still works as expected.

Find a student faster, at the front desk

  • Search by phone digits. Coaches and admins can now find a customer by typing the last few digits of a phone number into the student search (in addition to name and email). Useful when a customer hands the front desk a sticky note at the rink.

Private lessons now use the same booking emails as group courses

  • One consistent email. When a head coach books or reschedules an on-demand private (or semi-private) lesson, the customer now receives the same polished Enrollment Confirmed / Enrollment Updated email already used for group courses, complete with the session time and the one-tap Get Door Access Code link. No more two slightly different emails for the same kind of event.

Behind-the-scenes reliability work

  • Faster diagnosis when something does go wrong. Detailed, privacy-safe tracing has been added to the checkout return flow, so the very rare cases where a payment goes through but the booking screen doesn't fully load can be pinpointed and resolved much faster. No new personal data is stored about customers — the traces only record which step of the checkout flow was reached.
  • Server logs are now rotated automatically. Operational housekeeping so the platform keeps a clean, predictable disk footprint as traffic grows.
v1.2.4 June 21, 2026

A clearer teaching calendar for coaches

  • See lesson, coach, and students at a glance. Each event on the coach's teaching calendar now shows the lesson, the assigned coach, and the enrolled student(s) right on the card — no need to hover to find out who you're teaching.
  • Lesson types are color-coded. Private on-demand, semi-private on-demand, activities and camps, and regular group courses each have their own color and icon — so you can scan a day and instantly tell what's coming up.
  • Cleaner day view. The day grid now uses a soft sky-blue background so booked sessions stand out clearly against the open time slots.
  • Hover for the full picture. Hovering over any event brings up a tidy tooltip with the lesson, time, coach, full student list, and lesson type.

Reliability

  • Door access instructions now live inside the confirmation, update, and reminder emails. The old standalone Door Access Instructions email is no longer sent — customers were receiving two emails (a confirmation and a separate access email) for every booking and enrollment, which was easy to miss and easy to confuse. The same one-tap Get Door Access Code link is now embedded directly in the emails Open24 already sends, anchored to the relevant session and time window. Specifically, the Door Access section now appears in:
    • Your Booking Is Confirmed — immediately after a court is booked.
    • Enrollment Confirmed — immediately after enrolling in a course, activity, or camp.
    • Enrollment Updated — sent when a group course, activity, or camp session is rescheduled, or when an admin moves an enrollment to a different course. The Door Access section is new to this email in v1.2.4.
    • Your Private Lesson Is Scheduled — sent when a Head Coach books or reschedules an on-demand private/semi-private lesson on behalf of a student. (On-demand reschedules send only this email — never a duplicate Enrollment Updated.)
    • Reminder: Upcoming Booking — the pre-booking reminder.
    • Reminder: Upcoming Session — the pre-course-session reminder.
    One fewer email in the inbox, and the access link is always with the booking it belongs to.
  • Resource time slots save the right time, every time. Fixed a subtle bug where saving a resource time slot from outside the rink's timezone — or around a daylight-saving change — could shift the slot by an hour. Times now stay anchored to the rink's local time no matter where the admin is working from.
v1.2.3 June 18, 2026

Clearer email when staff add a pass to your account

  • A dedicated notification. When an Open24 staff member adds a pass to your account, you'll now receive a purpose-built email titled A Pass Has Been Added to Your Account. It accurately reflects your new balance and expiry — no more references to a payment amount or confirmation number that don't apply.
  • Honest balance, even with goodwill comps. If staff add a pass with a custom remaining-sessions count (for example, comping a partial balance), the email now shows exactly what's in your account.
  • Internal notes stay internal. Notes staff add for their own audit trail are no longer included in the customer email.
v1.2.2 June 17, 2026

Admin passes

  • Add a pass for any user. A new Add Pass button on the admin Passes page lets staff grant a pass directly to a customer's account — useful for complimentary passes, gestures of goodwill, or recovering a missed Stripe purchase — without a card transaction.
  • Sensible defaults, simple overrides. Unit count and expiry default to the chosen pass template; admins can override either with a live "Expires on…" preview, and add an internal audit note (up to 1,000 characters).
  • Choose whether the customer is notified. A "Send notification email" toggle (on by default) controls whether the recipient receives the standard Your Pass Has Been Added to Your Account email — uncheck it for silent back-fills so customers aren't double-notified after a recovery.
  • Transparent audit trail. Passes created by an admin now show a Granted by admin source badge with timestamp, plus the internal note, on the pass detail page — so the next admin can see exactly when and why a pass was added.

Reliability

  • Pass and gift-card purchases land in the database reliably. Fixed an edge case where a successful Stripe charge for a pass or a gift card could fail to create the corresponding record, requiring manual admin recovery. Both purchase paths are now hardened with clearer error logging so any future regression is diagnosable from server logs alone.
v1.2.0 June 16, 2026

Admin course management

  • Delete a course safely. Courses can now be deleted as soon as they have no active enrollments — old sessions, payments, waitinglist entries and reserved court slots are cleaned up automatically as a single transaction.
  • Clearer "can't delete" feedback. When a course can't be removed, the Delete button stays visible but disabled with an amber info icon. Hovering reveals a panel explaining exactly why (e.g. "This course has 3 active enrollments").
  • All Sessions view toggle. The session list now offers a Cards view (default) and a Table view for quick scanning. Your preference is remembered per browser.

Course checkout

  • Automatic recovery for interrupted payments. If your browser tab closes, your phone falls asleep, or your network drops between the moment your card is charged and the moment the confirmation screen loads, the system now finalizes your enrollment for you within seconds — no need to contact support. Your booking, confirmation email, and door access instructions all arrive normally.
  • Header navigation fixed on the checkout page. Clicking the Course link in the top navigation while on /enrollments/checkout previously sent customers to a broken /enrollments/enroll URL. It now reliably returns to the main /enroll browse page from anywhere in the app, so customers can step back out of checkout to keep shopping for courses without losing the navigation.
v1.1.7 June 14, 2026

Admin "Create User" overhaul

  • Admins no longer set passwords or activation flags. The form now collects just the basics (name, email, phone, optional Stripe ID); the new user is emailed an invitation link to sign agreements and set their own password.
  • The success toast now tells the admin whether the invitation email was actually delivered, so they know if a manual resend is needed.

Invitation email redesigned

  • Polished new layout — centered hero, personalised greeting (with a graceful "Hi there," fallback), clear "What to expect" steps, prominent CTA and a 7-day-validity reassurance.

Bug fixes

  • Admin-created users no longer end up with blank first/last names.
  • Stray template tokens (e.g. {{/if}}) no longer leak into email bodies.

On-demand requests

v1.0.0 June 8, 2026

First stable release

  • Head Coach role — Head Coaches now have full course-management parity with Admins (courses, cohorts, enrollments, sessions, attendances, waitlists, on-demand requests) plus a dedicated sidebar group and a new read-only Resource Calendar page polished to match the admin view.
  • New enrolments by a head coach are tagged as Enrolled by Head Coach end-to-end, distinct from admin enrolments.
  • Stripe checkout hardening — invalid session_id values are rejected up-front, stopping bot/prefetch noise hitting Stripe with placeholder URLs.
  • The wallet now labels migrated gift-card credits as From: System Migration instead of Unknown.
